# THE 11:11 INSPECTION
## Durren Laggan Incorporated — Episode 01

**Genre:** Psychological horror / Mystery / Suspense
**Format:** Cinematic Roblox animation
**Target duration:** 8–12 minutes
**Setting:** Bellwether, a fictional town controlled by Durren Laggan Incorporated.

> Draft 1 as written. See `review-notes.md` for continuity fixes and suggestions.

---

# SCENE 01 — THE THREE KNOCKS
**Time: 0:00–0:45**

VISUAL:

A dark screen.

A digital clock reads 11:10 PM.

Slowly, the camera moves through a quiet suburban house. There is almost no lighting except for a hallway lamp.

A teenage boy, Alex, stands near the front door.

His mother is behind him, holding his younger sister's hand.

The clock changes.

11:11 PM.

Three loud knocks.

KNOCK.

KNOCK.

KNOCK.

Silence.

A man's voice comes from outside.

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"Alex? It's Dad."

Alex freezes.

His mother immediately covers his mouth.

MARA:
"Don't."

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"Alex, please. I've been looking for you."

The camera moves towards the front door.

The door handle slowly turns.

CUT TO BLACK.

NARRATOR:

"In Bellwether, everyone follows one rule. At exactly 11:11 PM, you never open your front door. Not for your friends. Not for your family. And especially... not for someone you thought was gone."

TITLE CARD:

THE 11:11 INSPECTION

---

# SCENE 02 — A PERFECT TOWN
**Time: 0:45–2:00**

VISUAL:

Daytime aerial shot of Bellwether.

Beautiful houses. Clean roads. Children playing. A supermarket. A school. A large corporate building in the distance.

The camera slowly approaches a huge sign at the town entrance.

SIGN:

WELCOME TO BELLWETHER
A PROUD DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED COMMUNITY.

NARRATOR:

"Bellwether was supposed to be the perfect place to live. Six years ago, Durren Laggan Incorporated purchased the entire town and rebuilt almost everything."

"Every family received a house. Electricity was free. Water was free. Even healthcare was covered."

"But there was one condition."

VISUAL:

An employee in a grey company uniform hands residents a document.

NARRATOR:

"Every household had to sign a residential agreement."

CUT TO:

Alex eating breakfast with his mother Mara and sister June.

JUNE:
"Alex, are you coming to the park today?"

A loud vehicle passes outside.

A DLI maintenance truck stops in front of their house.

A company employee walks towards the front door and slides an envelope through the letterbox.

ALEX:
"What's that?"

MARA:
"Nothing important."

Alex picks it up.

CLOSE-UP:

DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED
ANNUAL RESIDENTIAL VERIFICATION
TIME: 11:11 PM
DATE: TODAY

ALEX:
"What's a residential verification?"

MARA:
"Just a routine inspection."

ALEX:
"At eleven at night?"

MARA:
"Alex. Don't ask questions."

The camera focuses on Mara's face.

She looks genuinely frightened.

---

# SCENE 03 — THE MISSING NAME
**Time: 2:00–3:15**

VISUAL:

Alex enters his mother's bedroom looking for a charger.

He accidentally opens an old drawer.

Inside is an old photograph.

It shows Mara, Alex as a younger child, June as a baby, and a man standing behind them.

Alex recognises the man.

ALEX:
"Wait... Dad?"

He takes the photograph downstairs.

ALEX:
"Mom, why did you hide this?"

Mara turns around.

MARA:
"Where did you find that?"

ALEX:
"Who is this?"

MARA:
"Your father."

ALEX:
"Then why do we never talk about him?"

Mara takes the photograph.

MARA:
"Because he left."

ALEX:
"When?"

MARA:
"I don't remember."

Alex notices something strange.

The man's face in the photograph is slowly fading.

Not physically. The printed photograph itself appears unusually faded.

NARRATOR:

"Alex had always believed his father abandoned the family. But that photograph made him question something."

"He could barely remember his father's voice. He couldn't remember his birthday. He couldn't even remember the last time they had spoken."

"Yet he was certain of one thing."

"He had not left."

ALEX:
"Mom... what happened to him?"

MARA:
"Forget it, Alex."

She places the photograph inside a locked drawer.

---

# SCENE 04 — THE OLD TECHNICIAN
**Time: 3:15–4:30**

VISUAL:

Alex walks through a quiet street.

He notices an old man working in a garage.

A sign outside reads:

HARLAN'S REPAIRS.

Alex approaches.

HARLAN:
"You're Mara's boy, aren't you?"

ALEX:
"Yeah. How do you know?"

HARLAN:
"I used to work for your father."

Alex stops.

ALEX:
"You knew my dad?"

Harlan looks around before responding.

HARLAN:
"Lower your voice."

ALEX:
"What happened to him?"

Harlan closes the garage door.

HARLAN:
"Your father worked for Durren Laggan. Not in maintenance. He worked in residential records."

ALEX:
"What does that mean?"

HARLAN:
"It means he knew who actually lived in this town."

ALEX:
"I don't understand."

HARLAN:
"Listen carefully. When the inspection starts tonight, do not open your door. No matter who is outside."

ALEX:
"Why?"

HARLAN:
"Because the people knocking aren't always the people you think they are."

Harlan gives Alex a small metal key.

HARLAN:
"Your father left something at the old substation. Take this. And don't tell anyone I spoke to you."

ALEX:
"Wait! What is DLI hiding?"

HARLAN:
"Everything."

CUT TO:

Harlan watching Alex walk away.

He turns towards an old CCTV monitor.

On the screen, a message appears:

RESIDENTIAL FILE DETECTED.
ALEX VALE.
STATUS: UNDER REVIEW.

Harlan immediately switches off the monitor.

---

# SCENE 05 — 11:11 PM
**Time: 4:30–6:00**

VISUAL:

Return to the Vale household.

The family is sitting together.

The television suddenly shuts off.

The lights flicker.

A loudspeaker outside activates.

DLI AUTOMATED VOICE:

"Attention, Bellwether residents."

"Annual residential verification will begin in sixty seconds."

"Please remain inside your registered properties."

Mara locks the front door.

She turns off the hallway lights.

JUNE:
"Mom, what's happening?"

MARA:
"Nothing. Just stay with me."

ALEX:
"Mom, I know something happened to Dad."

MARA:
"Not now."

The wall clock shows 11:10:59.

11:11 PM.

Three knocks.

KNOCK.

KNOCK.

KNOCK.

A man's voice.

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"Mara?"

Mara begins crying.

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"I know you're in there."

ALEX:
"Mom... is that Dad?"

MARA:
"Don't answer."

The voice changes.

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"Alex?"

Silence.

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"Alex, I know you remember me."

Alex slowly approaches the window.

He pulls back the curtain slightly.

A man is standing outside.

It looks exactly like his father.

But the man is wearing a grey DLI uniform.

There is a company badge on his chest.

He is holding a clipboard.

The man looks directly at Alex.

He smiles.

Then his lips move without any sound.

The CCTV monitor in the hallway switches on by itself.

TEXT:

RESIDENTIAL VERIFICATION FAILED.

UNREGISTERED MEMORY DETECTED.

CORRECTION AUTHORIZED.

The outside figure raises its hand and waves.

The lights switch off.

CUT TO BLACK.

---

# SCENE 06 — WHAT DLI REALLY DOES
**Time: 6:00–7:30**

VISUAL:

The following morning.

Alex wakes up.

He checks the photograph.

His father's face is almost completely gone.

He runs downstairs.

ALEX:
"Mom! Something happened last night!"

MARA:
"Alex, please. We need to talk."

ALEX:
"That was Dad outside!"

MARA:
"Alex... we never had a father in this house."

Alex stares at her.

ALEX:
"What?"

June enters.

JUNE:
"Who's Dad?"

Alex is devastated.

NARRATOR:

"By morning, his mother and sister had completely forgotten him."

"His photograph was gone. His name wasn't on any family document. Even the family contact list had changed."

"Except Alex remembered."

VISUAL:

Alex runs to Harlan's garage.

The garage is empty.

There is a DLI notice on the door.

PROPERTY VACATED.
RESIDENT COUNT: 0.

Alex uses the metal key on an old locked storage box.

Inside is a small recording device.

He presses PLAY.

FATHER'S RECORDING:

"If you're hearing this, I'm sorry."

"Alex, the company doesn't inspect houses."

"It inspects people."

"When DLI rebuilt Bellwether, they created something called the Resident Index. Every person in the town is stored in a company database."

"If someone becomes a problem, if they remember something they shouldn't, or if their identity doesn't match the records..."

"They get corrected."

PAUSE.

"The thing that comes knocking at 11:11 isn't the person you remember."

"It's the version DLI kept."

"Do not let them replace you."

The recording ends.

NARRATOR:

"Alex finally understood."

"Durren Laggan Incorporated wasn't protecting Bellwether."

"It was controlling it."

---

# SCENE 07 — THE OLD SUBSTATION
**Time: 7:30–9:00**

VISUAL:

Alex reaches an abandoned electrical substation on the outskirts of town.

He inserts Harlan's key.

The door opens.

Dusty computers. Old cables. A flickering emergency light.

A monitor turns on.

DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED.

RESIDENT INDEX — INTERNAL ACCESS.

Alex searches his father's name.

RESULT:

ELIAS VALE.
FORMER EMPLOYEE.
STATUS: REMOVED.

He searches his own name.

ALEX VALE.
RESIDENT STATUS: UNDER REVIEW.
REPLACEMENT MODEL: PREPARED.

Alex steps backwards.

A second monitor turns on.

A map of Bellwether appears.

Every house has a green light.

Some turn red.

ALEX:
"What's happening?"

The automated DLI voice activates.

"Unregistered access detected."

"Residential correction process will resume at 11:11 PM."

Alex notices another option on the computer:

RESTORE ORIGINAL RESIDENT RECORDS.

He clicks it.

ACCESS DENIED.

He finds a small audio file from his father.

FATHER'S RECORDING:

"Alex, if you've reached this place, there's still a chance."

"The company can change what people remember. But it cannot completely destroy the original records."

"The transmitter outside this building connects to every house in Bellwether."

"Use it."

"Make them remember."

The power goes out.

Alex hears footsteps in the hallway.

A familiar voice.

VOICE:
"Alex?"

It sounds like Harlan.

ALEX:
"Mr. Harlan?"

VOICE:
"Come outside. I can help you."

Alex looks through the small window.

A figure stands in the hallway.

It is Harlan.

But something is wrong.

The figure's face is expressionless.

Its company badge reads:

DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED.

RESIDENTIAL COMPLIANCE.

Alex slowly backs away.

---

# SCENE 08 — THE LAST BROADCAST
**Time: 9:00–10:30**

VISUAL:

Alex runs towards the transmitter.

A large digital clock on the substation wall reads 11:10 PM.

The DLI automated voice activates.

"Residential verification will begin in sixty seconds."

Alex connects the recording device to the transmitter.

A warning appears:

UNAUTHORIZED BROADCAST.

He presses CONFIRM.

The monitors flicker.

Across Bellwether, loudspeakers activate.

ALEX:
"My name is Alex Vale."

"I live in House 17."

"My father's name was Elias Vale."

"He worked for Durren Laggan Incorporated."

"He didn't leave us."

"They removed him."

CUT TO:

Different residents hearing the broadcast.

An old woman looks at a faded photograph.

A man opens an old drawer and finds a missing identity card.

A child remembers somebody who used to live upstairs.

BACK TO ALEX.

He continues.

ALEX:
"If you remember someone who disappeared... say their name."

The clock reaches 11:11 PM.

The substation door begins shaking.

Three knocks.

KNOCK.

KNOCK.

KNOCK.

VOICE OUTSIDE:
"Alex. Please open the door."

It is his father's voice.

The transmitter starts malfunctioning.

DLI AUTOMATED VOICE:

"Unauthorized memory restoration detected."

"Please terminate transmission."

Alex refuses.

ALEX:
"His name was Elias Vale!"

"His name was Elias Vale!"

The monitors suddenly turn green.

RESTORATION PROCESS INTERRUPTED.

The banging stops.

Silence.

The power returns.

The substation door slowly opens.

Nobody is there.

---

# SCENE 09 — A NEW EMPLOYEE
**Time: 10:30–11:30**

VISUAL:

The next morning.

Alex returns home.

Mara is looking at the old family photograph.

Her eyes fill with tears.

MARA:
"Alex..."

ALEX:
"Mom?"

MARA:
"Who is this man?"

She points to the photograph.

Alex smiles slightly.

ALEX:
"That's Dad."

Mara looks confused.

Then she quietly says:

MARA:
"I remember."

They embrace.

For the first time in years, the family remembers Elias.

NARRATOR:

"Alex thought he had won."

"He had restored the memories of the residents. He had stopped the inspection. And for the first time, Bellwether remembered the people Durren Laggan had erased."

"But DLI was still watching."

VISUAL:

Cut to a corporate office.

A mysterious executive sits behind a desk.

Only their hands are visible.

A computer displays:

DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED
INTERNAL RESIDENTIAL DATABASE.

BELLWETHER.
INSPECTION RESULT: UNRESOLVED.

ALEX VALE.
STATUS: ACTIVE.

EMPLOYEE ASSIGNMENT:
RESIDENTIAL COMPLIANCE DIVISION.

The executive picks up a telephone.

EXECUTIVE:
"Interesting."

Pause.

EXECUTIVE:
"He remembered everything."

Another pause.

EXECUTIVE:
"Prepare his orientation."

CUT TO BLACK.

TEXT ON SCREEN:

DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED.

YOUR HOME. OUR RESPONSIBILITY.

A final computer notification appears:

NEXT LOCATION IDENTIFIED.

END OF EPISODE 01.

A phone rings.

An unknown voice answers:

"Hello?"

A computer voice responds:

"Your residential verification is scheduled for tomorrow."
