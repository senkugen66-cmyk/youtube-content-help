# The 11:11 Inspection — Script Review (Draft 1)

The premise is strong: a company that "inspects people" rather than houses, a knock rule, and a copy of a lost parent at the door. The ending sets up a series well. Most of the notes below are continuity fixes. Each one is small, but together they decide whether viewers feel dread or confusion.

Runtime check: the scene timings add up to about 11:30, inside the 8–12 minute target.

---

## Must fix (continuity and logic)

### 1. The rule is never stated, so the threat is vague
Viewers need to know what happens if you open the door and what happens if you don't. Right now the family keeps the door shut and still gets "corrected" (Mara and June forget Elias), while Alex, the person actually flagged, is untouched. That makes the 11:11 rule feel optional.

**Suggested rule (say it once, in Elias's recording in Scene 06):**
> "If you open the door, they replace *you*. If you don't, they take what you were willing to let go of."

That one rule explains everything that happens:
- Mara had already half-given up on Elias, so her memory of him goes.
- June was a baby, so she has nothing to hold onto.
- Alex refuses to forget, so the Inspector can't touch him and he gets escalated to "Replacement model: prepared."

### 2. The cold open doesn't match Scene 05
Scene 01 is clearly a flash-forward of Scene 05, but the two disagree:

| Scene 01 | Scene 05 |
|---|---|
| Family standing by the door | Family sitting; Alex goes to the window |
| "Alex? It's Dad." / "I've been looking for you." | "Mara?" / "Alex, I know you remember me." |
| Door handle turns | Handle never turns |
| Mara covers Alex's mouth | No such beat |

**Fix:** put the Scene 01 beats into Scene 05 word for word: the lines, Mara's hand over Alex's mouth, and the handle turning. Then Scene 05 adds the new material (the window, the uniform, the monitor). Viewers will get the "oh, *this* is that moment" payoff.

### 3. Why doesn't a teenager know about an annual event?
The town is six years old and "everyone follows one rule," so Alex should have been through five inspections already. His "What's a residential verification?" breaks that.

**Fix:** make this year's envelope different. Address it to him:
> DURREN LAGGAN INCORPORATED
> INDIVIDUAL RESIDENTIAL VERIFICATION
> RESIDENT: ALEX VALE — HOUSE 17
> TIME: 11:11 PM

New line for Alex: *"Why does it have my name on it? It's never had my name on it."* That's scarier, it explains why Mara is frightened, and it connects to "ALEX VALE: UNDER REVIEW" on Harlan's monitor.

### 4. One key, two locks, and the substation clue sends Alex the wrong way
Harlan says "Your father left something at the old substation" and hands over the key. Alex then uses the key on a box in Harlan's garage (Scene 06), and uses it again on the substation door (Scene 07).

**Fix:** have Harlan say *"Your father left something for you. If anything happens to me, you'll find it where I keep the things DLI doesn't know about."* The key opens a box hidden under the garage floor (DLI cleared the garage but missed it). Inside are the recorder **and Elias's old DLI access badge**, which opens the substation. The substation then becomes a reveal instead of something Harlan already told us.

### 5. The on-screen text at the climax says the wrong thing
`RESTORATION PROCESS INTERRUPTED` means Alex *failed*. What the scene needs is the reverse.
**Fix:** `CORRECTION PROCESS INTERRUPTED`, then `ORIGINAL RECORDS RESTORED: 1,204 RESIDENTS`. A number makes the win feel big.

### 6. The substation layout is unclear in Scenes 07–08
Alex backs away from Harlan-copy *inside* the hallway, then "runs towards the transmitter" (which Elias said is *outside*), then knocks hit "the substation door" with Alex apparently inside again.

**Fix:** put the transmitter controls in an inner control room with a steel door. Alex runs in and locks it. The antenna is outside, seen through a window. Harlan-copy is in the hallway, and the 11:11 knocks come on the control-room door. Everything happens in one room, and the tension builds.

### 7. "Forgotten him" is ambiguous (Scene 06)
"By morning, his mother and sister had completely forgotten him" comes right after "Alex is devastated," so it reads as if they forgot *Alex*.
**Fix:** "…had completely forgotten his father."

### 8. The photo-fade description contradicts itself (Scene 03)
"Not physically. The printed photograph itself appears unusually faded." Pick one. Suggested direction: *the face is fading as he watches. Not old-photo fading: the face is being removed while the rest of the photo stays sharp.* In Roblox, tween the face decal's transparency across Scenes 03, 05 and 06 so it's a visible countdown.

## Should fix (stronger beats)

- **Set up the hallway monitor.** A "CCTV monitor in the hallway" turning on in Scene 05 comes out of nowhere. In Scene 02, show a DLI Home Panel by the Vales' front door glowing *"WELCOME HOME, VALE HOUSEHOLD (3 RESIDENTS)."* After Scene 08, it reads **4 RESIDENTS**. That's a free chill.
- **Mara's "We need to talk" (Scene 06)** suggests she knows something. Make the corrected Mara creepier and calmer: *"Alex, you were sleepwalking again. You kept saying a name."* Then: *"We never had a father in this house."*
- **Use Elias's real voice in the broadcast.** Alex has the recorder plugged into the transmitter, so let the town hear Elias's real voice under Alex's speech. The real father's voice over the speakers against the fake father's voice at the door is the emotional climax the episode is building to.
- **Let Mara say the name (Scene 09).** Instead of "That's Dad" → "I remember," have Mara get there herself: she looks at the photo and whispers *"…Elias."* Alex doesn't need to say anything.
- **The silent lip movement (Scene 05)** should pay off. Suggest Alex replays it in his head in Scene 08 and realises it said *"Open the door, son."* Or save it for Episode 02, but plan it now.
- **Leave one hook about Elias.** The episode never says whether Elias is dead, stored, or something else. In the executive scene, add one more line on the monitor: `ELIAS VALE — STATUS: REMOVED` flickers to `ELIAS VALE — STATUS: LOCATED`. That gives viewers a reason to watch Episode 02.
- **Harlan-copy appears before 11:11.** If the Inspectors only come at 11:11, the copy showing up at the substation earlier needs a reason. Easiest: Compliance staff are different from Inspectors. Inspectors knock, Compliance hunts. Say it once in Elias's second recording.
- **Harlan "worked *for* your father"**: a repair tech working for a records officer is odd. "I worked *with* your father" fits better.

## Roblox production notes

- **Expressionless face** for the copies: a blank or default face decal works well and is unsettling. Give every DLI copy the same blank face so viewers learn the visual rule.
- **Monitor text:** build every DLI screen with one SurfaceGui template (same font, same green-on-black or white-on-grey, same blinking cursor) so the corporate look stays consistent across all the screens.
- **The two fathers:** use the same voice actor for Elias and the copy. Run the copy through slight pitch-down and room-tone removal so it sounds a little *too* clean.
- **Knock sound:** use exactly the same knock sample every time (Scenes 01, 05, 08). Viewers will learn it by the second time.
- **The 11:10:59 → 11:11 clock flip:** hold on 11:10:59 for an uncomfortable 2–3 seconds before the flip. Cheap to do, and very effective.
