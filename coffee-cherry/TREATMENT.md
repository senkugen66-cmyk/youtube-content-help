# Ripening — a 10-second watercolor film

Style: Watercolor Brush (Lemo-Opuscar `watercolor`). 1920×1080, 10 s, English narration (warm female, Kokoro).

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Specimen plate that ripens (chosen).** A locked journal page: one coffee branch paints itself in, its cherries go green → yellow → red on the same view, a hand-written note labels the ripe one, then mist washes the page back to paper, leaving only one red cherry.
   *Why:* 10 seconds holds one idea and one transformation. The colour change *is* the story (palette as data / change in time on a locked view), and the single vermilion accent has an obvious home: the ripe cherry.
2. **Backwards painting.** Open on a finished cup of coffee, paint it backwards to a cherry. Strong, but a full reverse painting needs two complete scenes in 10 s — too crowded.
3. **Vertical tilt down a tall coffee shrub**, from flowers to cherries. Elegant but the scale read needs ≥ 15 s to land.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Before it's a cup, coffee is a cherry, ripening slowly on the branch.*
Setup: blank paper, a branch appears. Turn: green cherries blush to red. Ending: everything mists away except one ripe cherry — the beginning of a cup.

## 3. Benchmark — learn / don't learn

Learn: naturalist field-journal plates (specimen + hand note + leader line), generous paper margins.
Don't learn: any specific botanical plate's layout; no cute picture-book outlines; no vector lines.

## 4. Shot list (one continuous locked sheet)

| # | Time | Framing | Move | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–9.9 | Medium: one branch diagonally across the sheet, cherries clustered at leaf nodes | Slow push 1.00 → 1.10 toward the hero cluster | The observer leaning in as the fruit ripens |
| — | 7.4–9.9 | (same sheet) mist erase leaves the hero cherry | push continues | Chapter end: everything else fades, the one cherry remains |

## 5. Beat sheet

- 0.0–0.4 blank warm paper.
- 0.3–2.6 stem paints in (trunk profile, base → tip), then leaves pair by pair, structure before detail.
- 2.2–3.6 cherry clusters paint in, green.
- 3.6–6.6 ripening: green → yellow-green → amber → vermilion, cluster by cluster from branch base outward (staggered).
- 5.8–7.6 hand note "Coffea arabica" + "ripe cherry" with a pen leader line to the hero cherry; small mono label "≈ 15 mm".
- 7.4–9.2 paper-coloured mist rises and erases the branch and notes; the hero cherry stays.
- 8.6–9.9 title in serif italic under the cherry: "Every cup begins here."

## 6. Cue map

Tempo 72 bpm (0.833 s per beat), 3/4 feel, 4 bars ≈ 10 s. Felt piano + kalimba.
- Bar 1 (0–2.5): soft piano chord (Dmaj9 voicing), sparse.
- Bar 2 (2.5–5.0): kalimba notes land on each cluster appearing.
- Bar 3 (5.0–7.5): chord moves to Gmaj7; a kalimba note per cherry turning red.
- Bar 4 (7.5–10): resolve to D, single high kalimba note when the title appears; let ring.

## 8. Narration, subtitles, titles

Voice line (starts 1.0 s): "Before it's a cup, coffee is a cherry, ripening slowly on the branch."
Subtitles on the paper: centred bottom, serif italic ~36 px, ink ~78 % over a paper halo.
Notes in a handwriting face (Caveat); measure label in IBM Plex Mono; title in Cormorant Garamond italic.

## 9. As built (final timings after review)

- Length 10.2 s at 60 fps (the style favours 60 fps for the slow brush).
- Notes: "Coffea arabica" as the plate's heading, top left, 4.8–7.45 s; "ripe cherry" + "≈ 15 mm" with a leader to the hero cherry, 5.3–7.6 s.
- Mist erase 7.2–8.4 s; the camera pushes from 1.2× to ≈ 2.9× onto the one cherry during it.
- Title "It begins here." 7.8–10.2 s (shortened from "Every cup begins here." so it stays on screen long enough to read).
- Mix: foley ducked ≈ 8 dB and music ≈ 4 dB under the voice; music lifts 5 dB after the line ends. Mastered to −14 LUFS, −1.3 dBTP.
