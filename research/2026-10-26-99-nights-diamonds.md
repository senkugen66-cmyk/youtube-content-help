## 1. Game and video concept

**Roblox – 99 Nights in the Forest.** A tested guide to earning diamonds (the premium currency used to unlock classes) **without raiding the Cultist Stronghold**, suitable for solo players. Show a timed session and the actual diamond count.

Only make this video if you have a non-Stronghold method that works in the current version. The whole angle depends on it.

### Observed facts
- Most top results are about a year old; the game has had many updates since then (autocomplete lists alien, frog, owl, Christmas, Thanksgiving and Halloween updates).
- **No title in the top 20 promises a method without the Stronghold.** Most explicitly rely on it: "STRONGHOLD METHOD", "STRONGHOLD GLITCH", and chapter lists ending with "Cultist Stronghold Tips".
- Recurring wording: "500 DIAMONDS FAST", "fastest way to grind/farm", "best method", all caps.
- Code videos and script videos appear in the same results as method videos.

### Hypotheses (not proven)
- Zetthew's 14K probably comes partly from search, since the video ranks #4, but traffic sources aren't visible to us. View counts don't show that a title caused a video's success.
- Searchers who add "without stronghold" or "solo" get Stronghold-based results, which likely leaves them unsatisfied. That is the opening.
- Freshness may help ranking here because methods change with updates.

## 8. Recommended title

> **How to Get Diamonds Without the Stronghold in 99 Nights in the Forest**

Why:
- It contains the head phrase people type ("how to get diamonds … 99 nights in the forest") and the long-tail modifier ("without stronghold") from autocomplete.
- The difference from competitors comes early ("Without the Stronghold"), so it survives truncation on mobile.
- It answers the searcher's exact problem and promises something no visible competitor title does.
- It reads like a person explaining something. No all-caps, no "500 DIAMONDS FAST" template.
- If your measured rate is strong, use title #4 or add "Fast" instead. A real number is the strongest honest hook.

Supporting metadata: say "gems" as well as "diamonds" in the description and voiceover. Mention "solo" and "Cultist Stronghold" in the first two lines of the description. Add chapters per method.
